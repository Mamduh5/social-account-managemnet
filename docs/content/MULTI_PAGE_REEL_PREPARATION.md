It records the exact Page/Graph, claim, HTTPS source, exposed source date, observation date, expiry, inspection method and actual evidence. A model's claim that it verified a source is insufficient. Rai topic evidence requires a source date and expires within 24 hours; Developer evidence within seven days. These upper bounds do not make a stale topic relevant. Missing or insufficient evidence is a hold, not permission to fabricate facts.

It does not publish or create standing authority.
